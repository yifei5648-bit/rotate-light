# Rotate Light

Product × Interaction × Vibe Coding

Rotate Light is an interactive lighting concept based on three independently rotating layers. Physical rotation is translated into digital angle control and mood mapping.

## Concept

ROTATE → COMBINE → INTERACT

Users change the relationship between red, yellow, blue and white by rotating the three layers.

## Digital Extension

The physical interaction is mapped into a digital interface, allowing users to explore different compositions and mood states.

## Mood Mapping

- Calm
- Energetic
- Focused

Mood mapping is based on fixed interaction rules and does not use real-time AI.
